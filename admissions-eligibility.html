B.E. / B.Tech Eligibility – HSC (Academic Stream)

Candidates seeking admission to the 1st year of 4-Year B.E. / B.Tech degree courses through HSC (Academic) must meet the minimum average percentage in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) put together as mandated by the Government of Tamil Nadu & Anna University:

S.No Community / Category Minimum Average Marks in Mathematics, Physics & Chemistry (Put Together)
1 General Category (OC) 45.00 %
2 Backward Class (BC) including BCM 40.00 %
3 Most Backward Class & Denotified Communities (MBC & DNC) 40.00 %
4 Scheduled Caste / SC (Arunthathiyar) / Scheduled Tribe (SC / SCA / ST) 40.00 %
Note: Candidates from other states must possess equivalent qualification recognized by Anna University, Chennai.

B.E. / B.Tech Eligibility – HSC (Vocational Stream)

Pass in any one of the HSC (Vocational Subjects) with any one Engineering related subject (Mathematics, Physics, or Chemistry), with minimum average percentage in Related Subjects, Vocational Theory, and Practicals put together:

S.No Community / Category Minimum Average Marks in Related Subjects, Vocational Theory & Practicals
1 General Category (OC) 45.00 %
2 Backward Class (BC) including BCM 40.00 %
3 Most Backward Class & DNC 40.00 %
4 SC / SCA / ST 40.00 %

Post Graduate Eligibility (M.E. / M.Tech. / MBA / MCA)

S.No Programme Eligibility Requirement
1 M.E. / M.Tech. Programmes A pass in a recognized Bachelor's Degree (B.E. / B.Tech.) or equivalent Engineering degree from any recognized University obtaining at least 50% marks for Open Category (45% marks in case of reserved categories).
2 Master of Business Administration (MBA) A pass in a recognized Bachelor's Degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline and obtained at least 50% marks for Open Category (45% marks for reserved categories).
3 Master of Computer Applications (MCA) A pass in a recognized Bachelor's Degree of minimum 3 years duration with Mathematics at 10+2 level or Graduation level obtaining at least 50% marks (45% marks for reserved categories).
PG Selection Procedure:
Candidates are selected based on their performance in the common entrance examination (TANCET / CEETA-PG) conducted by Anna University / State Government. Candidates may also apply directly for Management Quota admission through the entrance test conducted on campus by SRM Valliammai Engineering College.
